How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Guatemala City

Headed to Guatemala City? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Guatemalan quetzal (GTQ), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in GTQ, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Guatemala. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Guatemalan quetzal in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Guatemala aren't blocked as suspicious.
