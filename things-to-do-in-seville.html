What to eat in Seville

Quick bite Espinacas con Garbanzos

A humble yet iconic tapas bar staple of spinach and chickpeas simmered with cumin and paprika, best mopped up with crusty bread.

Try it at Bodega Santa Cruz Las Columnas
Main Cola de Toro (Rabo de Toro)

A rich, slow-braised oxtail stew steeped in Seville's bullfighting heritage, so tender it falls off the bone.

Try it at Restaurante Las Piletas
Dessert Torrijas Sevillanas

Sevilla's beloved take on Spanish 'French toast,' soaked in wine or milk and fried till golden, especially cherished during Semana Santa.

Try it at Taberna El Miguelete

Getting to Seville

The main gateway is Seville Airport (SVQ). Arriving by train, the central station is Sevilla Santa Justa.
