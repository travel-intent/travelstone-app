What to eat in San Diego

Quick bite California Burrito

A San Diego original stuffed with carne asada, french fries, cheese, and guacamole, born to fuel late nights near the beach.

Main Fish Tacos (Baja-style)

Crispy battered fish, shredded cabbage, and creamy white sauce piled onto warm corn tortillas—San Diego's coastal claim to fame thanks to its border ties to Baja California.

Dessert Balboa Park Churros

Golden, cinnamon-sugar dusted churros enjoyed strolling through Balboa Park, a sweet nod to the city's deep Mexican-American heritage.

Don't miss in San Diego

  • Winter gray whale watching cruiseSan Diego's coastal waters are a prime migration corridor for Pacific gray whales, drawing boat tours each winter.
  • La Jolla Cove kelp forest & sea cave divingExplore vibrant kelp forests, leopard sharks, and sea caves in this protected marine reserve.
  • La Jolla Cove snorkelling with sea lionsSnorkel alongside playful sea lions and garibaldi fish in these clear, protected coastal waters.

Getting to San Diego

The main gateway is San Diego International Airport (SAN). Arriving by train, the central station is Santa Fe Depot.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in San Diego

Headed to San Diego? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in US dollar (USD), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in USD, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in United States. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little US dollar in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in United States aren't blocked as suspicious.
