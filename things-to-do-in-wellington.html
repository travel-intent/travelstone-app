Plump, sweet New Zealand mussels steamed open and served with a splash of local sauvignon blanc—an absolute Wellington seafood staple.

Getting to Wellington

The main gateway is Wellington Airport (WLG). Arriving by train, the central station is Wellington Station.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Wellington

Headed to Wellington? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in New Zealand dollar (NZD), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in NZD, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in New Zealand. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little New Zealand dollar in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in New Zealand aren't blocked as suspicious.
