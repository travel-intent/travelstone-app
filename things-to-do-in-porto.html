What to eat in Porto

Quick bite Bifana

A tender, garlicky pork sandwich soaked in spiced sauce, served in a crusty roll fresh from Porto's cafés and food stalls.

Main Francesinha

Porto's legendary sandwich stacked with meats and melted cheese, drenched in a rich beer-tomato sauce that's practically a religion in the city.

Dessert Toucinho do Céu

A dense, almond-rich 'bacon from heaven' cake with a golden egg-yolk sweetness that lingers long after the last bite.

Don't miss in Porto

  • Six Bridges Douro River CruiseGlide past Porto's iconic bridges and colorful riverside Ribeira district on a scenic Douro River boat tour.
  • Douro Valley Wine Region Boat TripSail upriver into the terraced vineyards of the Douro Valley, a UNESCO-listed wine landscape.

Getting to Porto

The main gateway is Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO). Arriving by train, the central station is Porto Campanhã.
