What to eat in Palma

Quick bite Ensaimada de Mallorca

This feather-light, spiral-shaped pastry dusted with powdered sugar is the irresistible taste locals grab fresh from the bakery each morning.

Main Tumbet Mallorquín

Layers of fried potatoes, eggplant and peppers smothered in a rich tomato sauce capture the heart of rustic Mallorcan home cooking.

Dessert Gató de Almendra with Almond Ice Cream

This dense, flourless almond cake paired with silky homemade almond ice cream showcases Mallorca's beloved almond harvest in every sweet bite.

Don't miss in Palma

  • Sailing the Bay of PalmaPalma's breezy bay and marina-studded coastline make it one of the Mediterranean's great sailing playgrounds.
  • Snorkelling the Mallorcan covesCrystal-clear calas just outside the city reveal rocky reefs and hidden swimming spots.
  • Day trips into the Serra de TramuntanaA short drive from Palma leads to dramatic mountain trails and UNESCO-listed terraced landscapes.

Getting to Palma

The main gateway is Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI).
