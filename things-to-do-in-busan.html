What to eat in Busan

Quick bite Ssiat Hotteok

Main Dwaeji Gukbap

Busan's soul-warming pork and rice soup, born from wartime resourcefulness and still slurped by locals at dawn or midnight.

Dessert Ssiat Hotteok

Busan's famous pumpkin-seed-stuffed sweet pancake, crispy outside and gooey with cinnamon-sugar syrup inside.

Don't miss in Busan

  • Hiking Geumjeongsan Mountain FortressTrek along ancient fortress walls with sweeping views over the city and coastline.
  • Busan Harbor Night CruiseGlide past the illuminated Gwangan Bridge and city skyline on an evening boat tour.
  • Scuba Diving near Oryukdo IsletsExplore rocky reefs and marine life just off Busan's iconic five-six islets.

Getting to Busan

The main gateway is Gimhae International Airport (PUS). Arriving by train, the central station is Busan Station.
