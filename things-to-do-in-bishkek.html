Don't miss in Bishkek

  • Trekking in the Ala Archa National ParkJust outside Bishkek, dramatic glacier-fed canyons and alpine peaks make for Central Asia's best quick mountain escape.
  • Horseback and yurt-stay trips into the Tien Shan foothillsRide out from the city toward nomadic pastures and overnight in traditional yurts under big mountain skies.

Getting to Bishkek

The main gateway is Manas International Airport (BSZ).
