What to eat in Hurghada

Quick bite Ta'ameya (Egyptian Fava Bean Falafel)

Crispy on the outside and herby-green inside, this Egyptian take on falafel made from fava beans is the ultimate grab-and-go bite along Hurghada's streets.

Main Grilled Red Sea Sayadeya (Fish with Spiced Rice)

Fresh catch straight from the Red Sea, pan-fried or grilled and served over fragrant cumin-spiced rice with caramelized onions—Hurghada's coastal specialty at its finest.

Dessert Om Ali

A warm, comforting Egyptian bread pudding soaked in sweetened milk and topped with nuts and raisins, the beloved finale to any Red Sea feast.

Don't miss in Hurghada

  • Red Sea scuba divingWorld-class coral reefs, wrecks, and crystal-clear visibility make Hurghada a top global diving destination.
  • Giftun Island snorkelling tripBoat out to vibrant coral gardens teeming with colorful reef fish in protected marine park waters.
  • Glass-bottom boat or Red Sea cruiseGlide over dazzling coral reefs and spot marine life without even getting wet.

Getting to Hurghada

The main gateway is Hurghada International Airport (HRG).
