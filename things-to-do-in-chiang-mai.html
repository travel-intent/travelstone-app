What to eat in Chiang Mai

Quick bite Sai Oua (Chiang Mai Sausage)

A fragrant grilled pork sausage packed with lemongrass, kaffir lime and herbs, sold sizzling at every street corner market.

Main Khao Soi

The city's beloved curry noodle soup, a rich coconut broth topped with crispy fried egg noodles that locals swear by.

Dessert Khao Niew Mamuang (Mango Sticky Rice)

Sweet sticky rice drenched in warm coconut cream alongside ripe golden mango, a Chiang Mai classic you can't leave without tasting.

Don't miss in Chiang Mai

  • Trekking in the Doi Inthanon highlandsMulti-day jungle treks through hill-tribe villages, waterfalls, and Thailand's highest peak.
  • Jungle camping with ethical elephant sanctuariesOvernight stays near rescued elephants in the forested hills outside Chiang Mai, a bucket-list wildlife encounter.
  • Exploring the Chiang Dao cave systemWander through atmospheric limestone caverns dotted with Buddhist shrines just north of the city.

Getting to Chiang Mai

The main gateway is Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX). Arriving by train, the central station is Chiang Mai Railway Station.
