What to eat in Wrocław

Quick bite Precel Wrocławski (Wrocław Pretzel)

A chewy, golden street pretzel often topped with cheese, bacon or sesame, perfect for snacking while strolling the Rynek.

Main Śląski Kluski z Modrą Kapustą i Rolada

Tender beef roulade served with grey Silesian potato dumplings and braised red cabbage, the ultimate hearty regional comfort meal.

Dessert Kremówka Wrocławska

A rich vanilla custard cream slice between layers of flaky puff pastry, beloved as the city's take on the famous Polish cream cake.

Getting to Wrocław

The main gateway is Wrocław Airport (WRO). Arriving by train, the central station is Wrocław Główny.
