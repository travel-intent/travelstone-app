What to eat in Cape Town

Quick bite Gatsby

A massive, overstuffed sandwich loaded with fries, meat and spicy sauce that Cape Town locals swear by for a hearty, indulgent bite.

Main Cape Malay Curry

A fragrant, mildly sweet-spiced curry rooted in the city's Cape Malay heritage, often served with yellow rice and sambals.

Dessert Malva Pudding

A warm, spongy apricot-infused pudding drenched in a sweet caramel sauce that's a beloved staple of Cape comfort food.

Don't miss in Cape Town

  • Hike Table MountainTrek up one of the New7Wonders of Nature for sweeping views over the city and coastline.
  • Cage diving with great white sharksCome face-to-face with the ocean's apex predator in the waters off nearby Gansbaai or False Bay.
  • Whale watching along the Cape coastSpot southern right whales breaching just offshore during the winter and spring migration season.

Getting to Cape Town

The main gateway is Cape Town International Airport (CPT).
