What to eat in Lisbon

Quick bite Pastel de Bacalhau

Golden, crispy codfish and potato fritters sold at kiosks across Lisbon, best enjoyed piping hot with a squeeze of lemon.

Try it at Casa Portuguesa do Pastel de Bacalhau
Main Bacalhau à Brás

Shredded salt cod scrambled with eggs, onions, and crispy matchstick potatoes—Lisbon's ultimate comfort dish, said to have a thousand variations.

Try it at Cais no Bacalhau
Dessert Pastel de Nata

Flaky, custard-filled tarts with a caramelized top, best eaten warm from the ovens of Belém where the original recipe was born.

Try it at Manteigaria

Don't miss in Lisbon

  • Tagus River sunset cruiseGlide past Belém's monuments and the 25 de Abril Bridge as the sky turns gold over the river.
  • Sintra hills and coastal cliff walksWander misty forested trails near fairy-tale palaces just a short ride from Lisbon.

Getting to Lisbon

The main gateway is Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS). Arriving by train, the central station is Lisboa Santa Apolónia.
