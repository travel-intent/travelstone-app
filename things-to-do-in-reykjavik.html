What to eat in Reykjavík

Quick bite Pylsur (Icelandic hot dog)

A lamb-beef-pork hot dog piled with crispy fried onions, remoulade and sweet mustard, famously devoured at Reykjavík's beloved street stand Bæjarins Beztu.

Try it at Bæjarins Beztu Pylsur
Main Plokkfiskur

A comforting mashed fish stew of cod or haddock with potatoes, onions and béchamel, beloved as classic Reykjavík home cooking.

Try it at Salka Valka eldhús/kitchen
Dessert Skyr

A velvety, protein-rich Icelandic dairy staple, often topped with fresh berries and cream for a tangy-sweet finish.

Try it at Krónan Hallveigarstíg

Don't miss in Reykjavík

  • Northern Lights tour from ReykjavíkChase the aurora borealis away from city lights on a guided night tour across the surrounding countryside.
  • Whale watching in Faxaflói BayHop on a boat straight from the Old Harbour for a chance to spot minke whales, dolphins, and even humpbacks.
  • Golden Circle & glacier/ice cave day tripUse Reykjavík as your base to explore nearby glaciers and natural ice caves on an unforgettable day excursion.

Getting to Reykjavík

The main gateway is Keflavík International Airport (KEF).
