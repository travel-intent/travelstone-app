What to eat in Naples

Quick bite Cuoppo Napoletano (fried seafood/street food cone)

A paper cone piled with crispy fried seafood, arancini and potato croquettes, perfect for eating on the go through Naples' alleys.

Main Pizza Margherita (Napoli-style)

Born in Naples, this soft, charred-crust pizza with San Marzano tomatoes, mozzarella and basil is the original and still the gold standard.

Dessert Sfogliatella

A shell-shaped, crisp-layered pastry filled with sweet ricotta and candied citrus that defines Neapolitan breakfast indulgence.

Don't miss in Naples

  • Mount Vesuvius crater hikeTrek to the rim of the infamous volcano that buried Pompeii, with sweeping views over the Bay of Naples.
  • Boat trip to Capri and the Blue GrottoSail across the bay to explore Capri's dramatic coastline and glide into the glowing waters of the Blue Grotto.
  • Path of the Gods on the Amalfi CoastA day trip from Naples to walk this cliffside trail with jaw-dropping views over the Tyrrhenian Sea.

Getting to Naples

The main gateway is Naples International Airport (NAP).
