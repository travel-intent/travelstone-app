How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Cartagena

Headed to Cartagena? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Colombian peso (COP), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in COP, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Colombia. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Colombian peso in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Colombia aren't blocked as suspicious.
