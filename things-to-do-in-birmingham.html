What to eat in Birmingham

Quick bite Chip Cob / Chip Barm

A humble but beloved Brummie snack of hot chips crammed into a soft bread roll, dripping with sauce and pure comfort.

Main Birmingham Balti

A sizzling, spice-laden curry cooked and served in its own flat-bottomed balti pan, born in the city's legendary Balti Triangle.

Dessert Cadbury Chocolate (Bournville)

Birmingham is the historic home of Cadbury, and a taste of their velvety chocolate straight from Bournville feels like a sweet pilgrimage.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Birmingham

Headed to Birmingham? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in pound sterling (GBP), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in GBP, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in United Kingdom. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little pound sterling in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in United Kingdom aren't blocked as suspicious.
