What to eat in Mangalore

Quick bite Mangalore Buns

Sweetish, fluffy deep-fried banana buns often paired with coconut chutney for a beloved teatime snack.

Main Kori Rotti

A coastal classic of crisp rice rotti soaked in fiery, coconut-based chicken curry that locals swear by.

Dessert Gadbad Ice Cream

A joyous, colourful layered sundae of multiple ice creams, jelly, fruits and nuts that's a Mangalore institution.

Getting to Mangalore

The main gateway is Mangaluru International Airport (IXE). Arriving by train, the central station is Mangaluru Central.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Mangalore

Headed to Mangalore? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Indian rupee (INR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in INR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in India. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Indian rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in India aren't blocked as suspicious.
