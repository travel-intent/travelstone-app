What to eat in Bucharest

Quick bite Covrigi

Chewy, freshly baked pretzel-like rings sold on nearly every Bucharest street corner, perfect for an on-the-go snack.

Main Sarmale

Tender cabbage rolls stuffed with spiced minced meat and rice, slow-simmered and served with a dollop of sour cream—Romania's national comfort dish.

Dessert Papanași

Golden fried or boiled dough dumplings crowned with sour cream and tart fruit jam, a beloved Romanian indulgence.

Getting to Bucharest

The main gateway is Henri Coandă International Airport (OTP). Arriving by train, the central station is Gara de Nord.
