  • Dive the Mesoamerican ReefExplore the second-largest barrier reef in the world, teeming with colorful coral and marine life just offshore.
  • Swim in a Yucatán cenoteDiscover the magical underground sinkholes near Cancún, perfect for swimming and snorkeling in turquoise freshwater.
