What to eat in Manali

Quick bite Siddu

This steamed wheat bun stuffed with walnut, poppy seed or paneer filling and served with ghee is Himachal's beloved mountain snack.

Main Trout Fish Curry / Himachali Dham

Fresh Beas river trout cooked in local spices, or the festive multi-course Dham thali, showcases Manali's mountain flavors best.

Dessert Sepu Vadi with Chana Madra sweetened touch / Angoori Gulab Jamun

Kullu-Manali feasts often close with rich, syrupy gulab jamuns that melt in the mouth after a hearty Himachali meal.

Don't miss in Manali

  • White-water rafting on the Beas RiverTackle the churning rapids of the Beas against a backdrop of pine-covered Himalayan slopes.
  • Snowmobiling in Solang ValleyRip across snowy slopes on a snowmobile with panoramic views of snow-capped peaks.
  • Trek to Hampta PassCross a dramatic high-altitude pass that links the lush Kullu valley with the stark landscapes of Lahaul.

Getting to Manali

The main gateway is Kullu Manali Airport (KUU).
