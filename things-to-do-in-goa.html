What to eat in Goa

Quick bite Ros Omelette

A fluffy Goan-style omelette drowned in spicy, thin meat gravy — a beloved late-night street snack found at local stalls across Panjim.

Try it at The Bobbys Ros Omlette
Main Goan Fish Curry Rice (Xitti Kodi)

Tangy, fiery kokum-and-coconut fish curry ladled over steaming rice — the everyday soul food that defines Goan homes and beach shacks alike.

Try it at Kokni Kanteen Panaji
Dessert Bebinca

A rich, layered Goan-Portuguese pudding baked layer by layer with coconut milk, eggs, and nutmeg, best enjoyed warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Try it at Fabs Bebinca

Don't miss in Goa

  • Mandovi River cruiseEvening cruises with live music and dance along the Mandovi River, a classic Goa night out.
  • Grande Island snorkelling and watersportsBoat out to Grande Island for snorkelling, dolphin spotting, and a mix of beach watersports.
  • Scuba diving off Grande IslandExplore shipwrecks and reef patches on introductory and certified dives in Goa's warm coastal waters.

Getting to Goa

The main gateway is Dabolim Airport (GOI). Arriving by train, the central station is Madgaon Junction.
