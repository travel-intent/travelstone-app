What to eat in Rome

Quick bite Supplì

Crispy fried rice balls oozing with molten mozzarella at the center, a beloved Roman street-side indulgence.

Main Cacio e Pepe

A deceptively simple pasta of tonnarelli tossed with Pecorino Romano and cracked black pepper, embodying Roman culinary genius.

Dessert Maritozzo

A soft, sweet bun split and stuffed with billowy whipped cream, cherished by Romans as the ultimate breakfast treat.

Getting to Rome

The main gateway is Ciampino–G. B. Pastine International Airport (CIA).
