What to eat in Copenhagen

Quick bite Smørrebrød

An open-faced rye bread canvas piled high with everything from pickled herring to roast beef, Copenhagen's edible art form.

Main Stegt Flæsk med Persillesovs

Denmark's beloved national dish of crispy pork slices smothered in creamy parsley sauce with boiled potatoes.

Dessert Kanelsnegle

A pillowy Danish cinnamon roll swirled with buttery spice, best enjoyed warm from a Copenhagen bakery window.

Don't miss in Copenhagen

  • Canal tour through Copenhagen's waterwaysGlide past Nyhavn's colorful facades and the Little Mermaid on a classic canal boat tour.

Getting to Copenhagen

The main gateway is Copenhagen Airport (CPH). Arriving by train, the central station is København H.
