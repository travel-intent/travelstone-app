What to eat in Zanzibar City

Quick bite Zanzibar Pizza (Forodhani style)

A deliciously messy stuffed street-food creation of minced meat or egg, veggies and cheese folded into thin dough and griddle-fried, best devoured hot at the Forodhani Gardens night market.

Main Zanzibar Octopus Curry (Pweza wa Nazi)

Tender octopus simmered low and slow in a fragrant coconut milk sauce spiced with cloves, cardamom and tamarind, showcasing the island's Swahili-Arab spice heritage.

Dessert Mkate wa Sinia

A rich, spiced rice-and-egg cake infused with cardamom and rosewater, traditionally baked for celebrations and adored for its dense, custardy sweetness.

Don't miss in Zanzibar City

  • Mnemba Atoll snorkelling tripGlide over vibrant coral gardens teeming with tropical fish just off Zanzibar's northeastern coast.
  • Traditional dhow sunset cruiseSail on a classic wooden dhow along the Stone Town coastline as the sky turns gold over the Indian Ocean.
  • Swimming with dolphins at KizimkaziHead out by boat to spot and swim alongside pods of wild dolphins in the warm coastal waters south of the city.

Getting to Zanzibar City

The main gateway is Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ).
