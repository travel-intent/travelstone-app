What to eat in Alexandria

Quick bite Alexandria-style Liver Sandwich (Kebda Eskandarani)

Spicy, garlicky sautéed liver stuffed into crusty baladi bread — a beloved late-night street food only Alexandria does quite like this.

Main Fresh Grilled Seafood (Samak Meshwi) at Alexandria's fish markets

Pick your catch straight from the Mediterranean and have it grilled with cumin, garlic and lemon at the city's iconic harborside fish restaurants.

Dessert Zabady bil Asal (Alexandrian-style honeyed yogurt) or Baleela sweets

A cool, honey-drizzled yogurt treat that locals turn to for a simple, comforting finish after a seafood feast by the sea.

Getting to Alexandria

The main gateway is Borg El Arab Airport (HBE). Arriving by train, the central station is Misr Station (Alexandria).

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Alexandria

Headed to Alexandria? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Egyptian pound (EGP), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in EGP, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Egypt. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Egyptian pound in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Egypt aren't blocked as suspicious.
