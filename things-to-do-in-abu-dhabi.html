What to eat in Abu Dhabi

Quick bite Shawarma

Thin-shaved marinated meat wrapped in soft khubz bread with garlic sauce and pickles, grabbed on nearly every Abu Dhabi street corner.

Main Machboos

Fragrant spiced rice piled high with tender chicken or lamb, slow-cooked with dried lime and warm spices — the Emirati national dish.

Dessert Luqaimat

Golden, crispy-on-the-outside dumplings drizzled with date syrup or honey, a beloved Emirati sweet treat.

Don't miss in Abu Dhabi

  • Dhow cruise along the CornicheGlide past the city skyline at sunset aboard a traditional wooden dhow.
  • Desert safari and overnight camping in the dunesBash over golden dunes by 4x4 then sleep under the stars at a Bedouin-style desert camp.
  • Scuba diving the artificial reefs and shipwrecksExplore surprisingly rich Gulf waters around sunken wrecks and reef structures.

Getting to Abu Dhabi

The main gateway is Zayed International Airport (AUH).
