What to eat in Cairo

Quick bite Ta'ameya (Egyptian Falafel)

Crispy, herb-packed fava bean fritters that Cairenes swear taste better than any other falafel in the world.

Main Koshari

Cairo's beloved carb-on-carb masterpiece of rice, lentils, macaroni, and crispy fried onions topped with tangy tomato sauce.

Dessert Umm Ali

A warm, comforting bread pudding drenched in milk, cream, and nuts, said to have royal Cairo origins.

Don't miss in Cairo

  • Desert safari into the Giza plateau dunesVenture beyond the pyramids into the Sahara sands for a night of stargazing and Bedouin-style camping.
  • Climb and explore the Great Pyramids area on footTrek around the Giza plateau and Sphinx, taking in millennia-old wonders up close.
  • Hot-air balloon flight over the Nile and pyramidsSoar above Cairo's skyline for a sweeping view of the Nile River and ancient monuments at sunrise.

Getting to Cairo

The main gateway is Cairo International Airport (CAI). Arriving by train, the central station is Ramses Station (Cairo).
