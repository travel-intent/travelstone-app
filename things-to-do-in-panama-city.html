Don't miss in Panama City

  • Panama Canal partial transit cruiseWatch massive ships rise and fall through the famous locks on a half-day canal transit tour.
  • Soberanía National Park rainforest hikeTrek through lush jungle just outside the city, spotting toucans, monkeys, and exotic flora.
  • Boat trip to the Pearl IslandsSpeed across the Bay of Panama to idyllic islands for beaches, fishing, and snorkeling.
