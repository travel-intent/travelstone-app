What to eat in Dubai

Quick bite Shawarma

Thinly shaved, marinated meat wrapped hot in flatbread with garlic sauce and pickles from a bustling Dubai stand.

Main Machboos

Emirati spiced rice piled with tender chicken or lamb, slow-cooked with dried lime and warm baharat spices.

Dessert Luqaimat

Golden, crispy-outside-fluffy-inside dumplings drizzled with date syrup or sweet sesame glaze.

Don't miss in Dubai

  • Desert safari with dune bashing & Bedouin campRace over red dunes in a 4x4 then unwind at a desert camp under the stars.
  • Hot-air balloon over the Dubai desertDrift above the dunes at sunrise for sweeping views of the desert and distant skyline.
  • Scuba diving at the artificial reefs & wrecksExplore man-made reefs and shipwrecks in the warm waters of the Arabian Gulf.
