What to eat in Bath

Quick bite Bath Bun

A rich, sugar-topped sweet bun studded with candied peel and crushed sugar nibs, best enjoyed still warm from a historic bakery.

Main Bath Chap with local ale-battered fish

Traditionally cured and cooked pig's cheek, a hearty West Country classic once beloved by Bath's working class and still served in gastropubs today.

Dessert Sally Lunn's Bun

A giant, pillowy brioche-like bun served warm with sweet or savoury toppings, baked to a secret recipe since the 1680s in Bath's oldest house.

Getting to Bath

The main gateway is Bristol Airport (BRS). Arriving by train, the central station is Bath Spa.
