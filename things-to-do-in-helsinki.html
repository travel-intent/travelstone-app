What to eat in Helsinki

Quick bite Lihapiirakka

A deep-fried meat pasty piled with mustard, ketchup and toppings, grabbed from Helsinki's late-night kiosks and market stalls.

Main Kalakukko

A hearty rye bread loaf stuffed with fish and pork fat, baked low and slow until wonderfully tender inside its crusty shell.

Dessert Runebergintorttu (Runeberg Torte)

A rum-soaked almond cake capped with raspberry jam and a sugary ring, adored across Finland every February.

Don't miss in Helsinki

  • Suomenlinna sea fortress boat tripHop on a short ferry across the harbour to explore the UNESCO-listed island fortress.
  • Archipelago island-hoppingCruise through Helsinki's stunning coastal archipelago, weaving between hundreds of small islands.

Getting to Helsinki

The main gateway is Helsinki-Vantaa Airport (HEL). Arriving by train, the central station is Helsinki Central Station.
