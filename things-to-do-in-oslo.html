What to eat in Oslo

Quick bite Pølse i lompe

A steaming Norwegian hot dog wrapped in a soft potato flatbread, grabbed from street carts all across Oslo.

Main Fårikål

Norway's national dish—a comforting stew of mutton and cabbage slow-simmered with whole peppercorns, deeply tied to Oslo's autumn traditions.

Dessert Krumkake

A delicate, cone-shaped waffle cookie often filled with whipped cream, cherished as a beloved Norwegian holiday treat.

Don't miss in Oslo

  • Oslomarka forest and lake hikingVast network of forested trails and lakes right at the city's edge, perfect for a day of fresh-air hiking.
  • Oslofjord boat cruiseSail among the islands and skerries of the Oslofjord for classic views of the city skyline from the water.

Getting to Oslo

The main gateway is Oslo Airport, Gardermoen (OSL). Arriving by train, the central station is Oslo Central Station.
