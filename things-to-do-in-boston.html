What to eat in Boston

Quick bite Lobster Roll

Sweet, buttery chunks of fresh lobster piled into a toasted split-top bun—New England's ultimate handheld indulgence.

Main New England Clam Chowder

A creamy, briny bowlful of tender clams and potatoes that's been warming Bostonians since the colonial era.

Dessert Boston Cream Pie

The city's official dessert—layers of sponge cake, silky custard, and a glossy chocolate glaze that inspired doughnuts worldwide.

Getting to Boston

The main gateway is Logan International Airport (BOS). Arriving by train, the central station is Boston South Station.
