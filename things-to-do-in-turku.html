What to eat in Turku

Quick bite Vety / Ateriaperunat (Turku's Vety sandwich)

Turku's own hearty street-food invention piling a whole egg onto a rye 'Jaffa' sandwich stacked with ham, cheese and pickles - a true local institution since the 1970s.

Main Kalakukko

Though rooted in eastern Finland, this rye-crusted fish and pork bake is a beloved hearty staple served across Turku's traditional taverns and markets.

Dessert Viinimarjapiirakka / Mustikkapiirakka (Turku-style berry pie)

A humble Finnish berry pie, often served warm with vanilla sauce, that Turku locals cherish as a cosy taste of summer.

Getting to Turku

The main gateway is Turku Airport (TKU). Arriving by train, the central station is Turku Central Station.
