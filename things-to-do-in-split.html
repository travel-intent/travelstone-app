What to eat in Split

Quick bite Burek

Flaky, coiled pastry stuffed with minced meat or cheese, grabbed hot from a bakery window for an on-the-go Split breakfast.

Main Pašticada

Tender beef slow-braised in a rich sweet-and-sour wine and prune sauce, served over homemade gnocchi for Sunday-best Dalmatian comfort.

Dessert Rožata

A silky Dalmatian egg custard flavored with rose liqueur, caramelized on top like a local twist on crème caramel.

Don't miss in Split

  • Blue Cave & Hvar island-hopping speedboat tourZip across the Adriatic to swim in the glowing Blue Cave and explore the lavender-scented island of Hvar.
  • Snorkelling the Adriatic coves near SplitExplore crystal-clear turquoise waters and hidden coves along the Dalmatian coastline.
  • Hiking Marjan Hill and the Biokovo mountain rangeClimb forested trails above Split for sweeping views over the city, islands, and sparkling sea.

Getting to Split

The main gateway is Split Airport (SPU). Arriving by train, the central station is Split Station.
