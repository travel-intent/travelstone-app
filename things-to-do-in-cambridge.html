What to eat in Cambridge

Quick bite Chelsea Bun (Fitzbillies)

A gloriously sticky, cinnamon-and-currant spiral bun that's been a Cambridge institution since 1921.

Main Cambridge Burnt Chorizo

A beloved pub classic pairing pan-charred chorizo with crusty bread, born in a Cambridge kitchen and now a staple on college-town menus.

Dessert Cambridge Burnt Cream

Cambridge's own claim to crème brûlée, its shattering caramel crust said to have been invented at Trinity College.

Getting to Cambridge

The main gateway is London Stansted Airport (STN). Arriving by train, the central station is Cambridge Station.
