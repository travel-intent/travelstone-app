What to eat in Bengaluru

Quick bite Masala Dosa (Davanagere/Bengaluru style with red chutney)

A crackling golden crepe stuffed with spiced potato mash, served with coconut chutney and sambar - the breakfast icon of the city's legendary darshinis.

Main Bisi Bele Bath

A fiery, tangy rice-lentil-vegetable one-pot dish loaded with spices and ghee that Bengalureans consider ultimate comfort food.

Dessert Mysore Pak

A melt-in-the-mouth ghee-soaked sweet born just down the road in Mysore, but a Bengaluru mithai shop staple loved by every local sweet tooth.

Getting to Bengaluru

The main gateway is Kempegowda International Airport (BLR). Arriving by train, the central station is KSR Bengaluru City Junction.
