What to eat in Malmö

Quick bite Räksmörgås

An open-faced shrimp sandwich piled high with hand-peeled shrimp, egg, mayo and dill is the quintessential quick harbour-side bite in Malmö.

Main Flæskesteg-inspired Skånsk kalops or Ål (eel) & potatoes

Malmö's proximity to the sea and Danish border makes fried or smoked eel served with new potatoes and creamy dill sauce a cherished Skåne classic.

Dessert Spettekaka

This towering, spit-roasted egg-and-sugar cake with its crisp, cone-like ridges is a Skåne specialty rooted in Malmö's own baking tradition.

Getting to Malmö

The main gateway is Malmö Airport (MMX). Arriving by train, the central station is Malmö Central Station.
