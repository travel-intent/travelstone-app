What to eat in Singapore

Quick bite Satay

Smoky skewers of grilled meat char-grilled over charcoal and dunked in a rich peanut sauce, a beloved hawker-stall classic.

Main Hainanese Chicken Rice

Silky poached chicken over fragrant rice cooked in its own stock, served with a fiery chilli-ginger dip that Singaporeans consider their national dish.

Dessert Chendol

A refreshing mound of shaved ice, coconut milk, palm sugar syrup and pandan jelly noodles that cools you down after a hot day out.

Getting to Singapore

The main gateway is Singapore Changi Airport (SIN).
