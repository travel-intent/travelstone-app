What to eat in Tampere

Quick bite Mustamakkara

Tampere's beloved black sausage, traditionally eaten with lingonberry jam and a glass of milk or sima at spring markets.

Main Tampere Mustamakkara with Silakat (fried Baltic herring)

A hearty local plate pairing the city's famous black sausage with crispy pan-fried Baltic herring, showcasing Tampere's working-class food heritage.

Dessert Munkki (Finnish doughnut) with Tampere-style coffee

Sugar-dusted, deep-fried Finnish doughnuts enjoyed warm from lakeside cafés, a comforting sweet treat locals swear by.

Getting to Tampere

The main gateway is Tampere-Pirkkala Airport (TMP). Arriving by train, the central station is Tampere Station.
