What to eat in Dubrovnik

Quick bite Peka

Though often a main, small peka-style snacks with octopus or veal slow-cooked under a bell-shaped lid are a must-try street favorite.

Main Dubrovačka Šporka Manistra

This hearty pasta and vegetable stew is a proud Dubrovnik classic, rich with slow-cooked flavors of the Dalmatian coast.

Dessert Rozata

This caramel custard, similar to flan, is Dubrovnik's beloved dessert, often infused with a hint of rose liqueur for a floral twist.

Don't miss in Dubrovnik

  • Dubrovnik city walls and Old Harbour boat cruiseSail past the iconic red-roofed Old Town and ancient fortifications for the classic postcard view of the Adriatic coast.
  • Kayaking and snorkelling around the city walls and Lokrum IslandPaddle beneath the towering medieval ramparts and explore hidden sea caves before snorkelling in the crystal-clear waters off Lokrum.
  • Hike or cable car up Mount SrđClimb the switchback trail above the Old Town for sweeping views over the terracotta rooftops and the shimmering Adriatic Sea.

Getting to Dubrovnik

The main gateway is Dubrovnik Airport (DBV).
