What to eat in Vadodara

Quick bite Sev Usal

A beloved Vadodara street breakfast of spicy sprouted moth bean curry piled high with crunchy sev, pav on the side, best enjoyed piping hot from a roadside stall.

Try it at JAY MAHAKALI SEV USAL CENTER KIRTISTAMBHWALA SUBHANPURA BRANCH
Main Vadodara-style Undhiyu

A hearty medley of winter vegetables, beans and fenugreek dumplings slow-cooked in oil and spices, celebrated as one of Gujarat's most iconic mixed vegetable dishes.

Try it at Shree Kathiyawadi Khadki
Dessert Malpua with Rabdi

Sweet, syrup-soaked pancakes topped with thick creamy rabdi make for a decadent Gujarati indulgence beloved in Vadodara's sweet shops.

Try it at Rabdiwala Corner ®

Getting to Vadodara

The main gateway is Vadodara Airport (BDQ). Arriving by train, the central station is Vadodara Junction.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Vadodara

Headed to Vadodara? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Indian rupee (INR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in INR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in India. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Indian rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in India aren't blocked as suspicious.
