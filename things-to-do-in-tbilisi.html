Don't miss in Tbilisi

  • Trek through Tbilisi's surrounding hills and gorgesHike up to Narikala Fortress or into the Tbilisi National Park for sweeping views over the old city and the Kura River.
  • Day trip to Vardzia or nearby cave sitesExplore ancient cave monasteries carved into cliffs, a dramatic half-day escape from the capital.

Getting to Tbilisi

The main gateway is Tbilisi International Airport (TBS). Arriving by train, the central station is Tbilisi Central Station.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Tbilisi

Headed to Tbilisi? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Georgian lari (GEL), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in GEL, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Georgia. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Georgian lari in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Georgia aren't blocked as suspicious.
