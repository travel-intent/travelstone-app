What to eat in Hiroshima

Quick bite Kaki (Grilled Oysters)

Plump, briny oysters from Hiroshima Bay grilled to smoky perfection right at the seaside stalls.

Main Hiroshima-yaki (Hiroshima-style Okonomiyaki)

Layers of cabbage, noodles, pork and egg griddled together into a savory masterpiece unique to the city.

Dessert Momiji Manju

A maple-leaf-shaped cake with sweet fillings like red bean or custard, sold warm around Miyajima.

Getting to Hiroshima

The main gateway is Hiroshima Airport (HIJ). Arriving by train, the central station is Hiroshima Station.
