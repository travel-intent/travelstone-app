Don't miss in Kathmandu

  • Gateway trek to the Everest & Annapurna regionsKathmandu is the launch point for legendary Himalayan treks, with flights to Lukla and trailheads to Everest Base Camp or the Annapurna Circuit.
  • Mountain flight over the HimalayasEarly-morning scenic flights from Kathmandu offer jaw-dropping views of Everest and other 8,000m peaks without setting foot on the trail.
  • Day trip to the Chobar Gorge and cavesJust outside the city, explore limestone caves and gorges steeped in Kathmandu Valley legend and geology.

The main gateway is Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM).

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Kathmandu

Headed to Kathmandu? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Nepalese rupee (NPR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in NPR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Nepal. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Nepalese rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Nepal aren't blocked as suspicious.
