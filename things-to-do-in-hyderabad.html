What to eat in Hyderabad

Quick bite Mirchi Bajji

Crispy, spicy battered green chillies stuffed with tangy fillings, best enjoyed piping hot from a street stall with chai.

Main Hyderabadi Biryani

Fragrant basmati rice slow-cooked in a sealed handi with marinated meat and saffron, the crown jewel of Hyderabadi cuisine.

Dessert Qubani Ka Meetha

A luscious stewed apricot dessert served with a swirl of fresh cream, a royal Hyderabadi finale to any meal.

Getting to Hyderabad

The main gateway is Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (HYD). Arriving by train, the central station is Secunderabad Junction.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Hyderabad

Headed to Hyderabad? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Indian rupee (INR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in INR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in India. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Indian rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in India aren't blocked as suspicious.
