What to eat in Varanasi

Quick bite Kachori Sabzi

Crispy, flaky kachoris served with a spicy potato curry, best enjoyed hot at a bustling Varanasi galli in the early morning.

Try it at The Ram Bhandar
Main Tamatar Chaat

A tangy, spicy tomato-based street delicacy simmered to perfection, unique to Varanasi's chaat culture and beloved by locals as a savory main snack.

Try it at Deena Chaat Bhandar
Dessert Malaiyo

An ethereal, saffron-tinted milk foam dessert that appears only in winter mornings, melting magically on your tongue.

Try it at Shreeji - Sweets & Milk

Getting to Varanasi

The main gateway is Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS). Arriving by train, the central station is Varanasi Junction.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Varanasi

Headed to Varanasi? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Indian rupee (INR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in INR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in India. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Indian rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in India aren't blocked as suspicious.
