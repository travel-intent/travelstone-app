Don't miss in Perth

  • Snorkelling with whale sharks or wreck diving at Rottnest IslandCrystal-clear waters around Rottnest Island offer superb snorkelling among reefs, shipwrecks, and even resident quokkas on the shore.
  • Swan River cruise to the Swan ValleyGlide past Perth's skyline and riverside parks on a relaxed cruise linking the city to the historic wineries of the Swan Valley.
  • Whale watching off Perth's coastEach year humpback and southern right whales migrate along the coastline, making for thrilling boat-based whale-watching tours.

Getting to Perth

The main gateway is Perth International Airport (PER).
