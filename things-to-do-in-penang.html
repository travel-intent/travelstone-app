Don't miss in Penang

  • Pantai Kerachut snorkellingExplore shallow reefs near Monkey Beach inside Penang National Park, reachable only by boat or jungle trail.
  • Penang mangrove & fireflies river cruiseGlide through coastal mangroves at dusk to spot twinkling fireflies and local wildlife.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Penang

Headed to Penang? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Malaysian ringgit (MYR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in MYR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Malaysia. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Malaysian ringgit in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Malaysia aren't blocked as suspicious.
