What to eat in Mumbai

Quick bite Vada Pav

Mumbai's legendary spicy potato fritter sandwiched in a soft bun with tangy chutneys, the ultimate on-the-go street food icon.

Main Bombay Duck Fry (Bombil Fry)

A crispy, spiced fried delicacy made from the city's iconic lizardfish, a true taste of Mumbai's coastal Koli heritage.

Dessert Falooda

Mumbai's beloved chilled dessert drink layered with vermicelli, basil seeds, jelly, and rose syrup, often topped with a scoop of ice cream.

Getting to Mumbai

The main gateway is Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM). Arriving by train, the central station is Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus.
