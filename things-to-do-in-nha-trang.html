What to eat in Nha Trang

Quick bite Bánh Căn

Crispy little rice-batter cakes cooked in clay molds and served with fresh herbs, scallion oil, and a tangy fish sauce dip - a beloved Nha Trang breakfast street food.

Main Bún Chả Cá Nha Trang

A vibrant rice noodle soup starring springy grilled and fried fish cakes in a clean, sweet-savory fish broth that showcases the city's coastal bounty.

Dessert Yến Sào (Bird's Nest Dessert)

A silky, delicately sweet swiftlet's nest soup that Nha Trang is famously renowned for, prized as a luxurious local treat.

Don't miss in Nha Trang

  • Hon Mun Island snorkellingExplore Vietnam's first marine protected area with colorful coral reefs and tropical fish.
  • Scuba diving around Nha Trang Bay islandsVietnam's top dive hub, offering reef dives and PADI courses amid warm, clear waters.
  • Four Islands boat tourHop between Nha Trang's scenic islands by boat, stopping for swimming, seafood, and island beaches.

Getting to Nha Trang

The main gateway is Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR). Arriving by train, the central station is Nha Trang Station.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Nha Trang

Headed to Nha Trang? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Vietnamese đồng (VND), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in VND, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Vietnam. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Vietnamese đồng in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Vietnam aren't blocked as suspicious.
