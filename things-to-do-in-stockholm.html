What to eat in Stockholm

Quick bite Toast Skagen

A beloved open-faced shrimp toast piled with dill-and-mayo-dressed shrimp and a dollop of roe, often enjoyed as a quick lunch or starter in Stockholm's bistros.

Main Köttbullar med potatismos

Sweden's iconic meatballs served with creamy mashed potatoes, tangy lingonberry jam, and rich cream sauce—comfort food locals swear by.

Dessert Kanelbulle

The classic Swedish cinnamon bun, warmly spiced and pearl-sugar topped, is the soul of Stockholm's cherished fika tradition.

Don't miss in Stockholm

  • Archipelago boat tourCruise through Stockholm's stunning 30,000-island archipelago, a quintessential way to see the city's watery surroundings.
  • Baltic Sea ferry cruiseHop aboard an overnight cruise ship to Helsinki or Åland islands, a classic Stockholm maritime tradition.
  • Kayaking among the islandsPaddle a kayak through calm channels between forested skerries just minutes from the city center.

Getting to Stockholm

The main gateway is Stockholm Arlanda Airport (ARN). Arriving by train, the central station is Stockholm Central Station.
