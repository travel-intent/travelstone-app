What to eat in Glasgow

Quick bite Scotch Pie

A double-crust hand-held pie of spiced minced mutton, sold hot from bakeries and beloved as Glasgow's classic on-the-go bite.

Main Glasgow-style Chicken Tikka Masala

Legend credits this creamy, tomato-and-spice curry to a Glasgow kitchen, making it the city's most famous "local" main course.

Dessert Deep-fried Mars Bar

A cheeky chip-shop classic - a chocolate bar battered and fried until molten, pure only-in-Scotland indulgence.

Getting to Glasgow

The main gateway is Glasgow Airport (GLA). Arriving by train, the central station is Glasgow Central.
