What to eat in Antalya

Quick bite Hibeş

This tahini-and-spice dip from Antalya's Arab-influenced coast is scooped up with fresh bread as a beloved street-side snack.

Main Antalya Piyazı with Tandır Kebabı

Tender slow-cooked tandır lamb paired with Antalya's uniquely tahini-dressed piyaz bean salad is a true regional classic.

Dessert Şekerpare

A syrup-soaked semolina cookie whose golden sweetness makes it an Antalya coffeehouse favorite.

Don't miss in Antalya

  • Turquoise Coast boat tripSail the sparkling bays near Antalya's old harbour and cliffside coastline aboard a traditional gulet.
  • Mediterranean scuba divingExplore underwater caves, reefs and shipwrecks along Antalya's clear turquoise waters.

Getting to Antalya

The main gateway is Antalya Airport (AYT).
