What to eat in Kandy

Quick bite Kottu Roti

The rhythmic clang of metal blades chopping godamba roti with egg, veggies and spices on a sizzling griddle is Kandy's iconic street-food soundtrack.

Try it at Gimanhala ගිමන්හල
Main Kandyan Rice and Curry

A soul-warming spread of red rice with multiple coconut-based curries, dried fish, and mallung, reflecting the hill capital's rich culinary heritage.

Try it at Rice And Curry Corporation
Dessert Kavum with Kithul Treacle

These deep-fried oil cakes drizzled with golden kithul palm syrup are a beloved traditional sweet rooted in Kandyan culture.
