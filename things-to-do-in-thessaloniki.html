What to eat in Thessaloniki

Quick bite Bougatsa

A warm, flaky pastry filled with sweet semolina custard (or savory cheese/mince), dusted with sugar and cinnamon and beloved as Thessaloniki's classic breakfast street food.

Main Bakaliaros Skordalia

Crispy golden fried salt-cod served with a punchy garlic-potato skordalia dip, a Thessaloniki taverna staple loved for its bold, comforting flavors.

Dessert Trigona Panoramatos

Crunchy triangular pastry cones from the Panorama district, soaked in syrup and filled with rich custard cream — an irresistible local sweet.

Getting to Thessaloniki

The main gateway is Thessaloniki Macedonia International Airport (SKG).
