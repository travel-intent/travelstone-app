What to eat in Colombo

Quick bite Kottu Roti

The rhythmic clang of metal blades chopping shredded roti with egg, veggies and meat on a sizzling griddle is Colombo's street-food soundtrack.

Try it at Kolamba Kade
Main Sri Lankan Crab Curry (Colombo-style)

Plump lagoon crabs simmered in a fiery, tamarind-tinged coconut curry are the city's proudest culinary flex.

Try it at Ceylon Curry Club
Dessert Watalappan

This glossy steamed coconut-jaggery custard, fragrant with cardamom and cashews, is Colombo's beloved festive sweet.

Try it at Royal Watalappan

Getting to Colombo

The main gateway is Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB). Arriving by train, the central station is Colombo Fort.
