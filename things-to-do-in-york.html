What to eat in York

Quick bite Yorkshire Parkin

A sticky, ginger-and-treacle oat cake traditionally nibbled as a quick, warming snack around Bonfire Night.

Main Yorkshire Pudding with Roast Beef

The county's proudest export—crisp, golden batter puddings cradling rich gravy-soaked roast beef.

Dessert Yorkshire Curd Tart

A creamy, nutmeg-spiced curd-cheese tart that's a beloved centuries-old Yorkshire classic.

Getting to York

The main gateway is Leeds Bradford Airport (LBA). Arriving by train, the central station is York Station.
