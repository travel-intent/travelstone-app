How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Doha

Headed to Doha? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Qatari riyal (QAR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in QAR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Qatar. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Qatari riyal in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Qatar aren't blocked as suspicious.
