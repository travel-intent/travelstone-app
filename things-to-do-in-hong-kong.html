What to eat in Hong Kong

Quick bite Egg Waffles (Gai Daan Jai)

Golden, bubbly egg puffs cooked in a honeycomb-shaped pan, crispy outside and soft inside — a beloved street-side treat.

Main Soy Sauce Chicken (See Yau Gai) with Char Siu

Glossy, fragrant poultry and caramelized barbecued pork hanging in shopfront windows are the soul of Hong Kong's roast meat culture.

Dessert Mango Sago with Pomelo (Yang Zhi Gan Lu)

A chilled, creamy mango and pomelo sago dessert that's the ultimate refreshing finish to a Cantonese meal.

Don't miss in Hong Kong

  • Dragon's Back trailHong Kong's famous ridgeline hike with sweeping views over Shek O and the coastline, often ranked among Asia's best urban hikes.
  • Victoria Harbour junk boat cruiseSail past the iconic skyline on a traditional red-sailed junk, best at sunset as the Symphony of Lights begins.

Getting to Hong Kong

The main gateway is Hong Kong International Airport (HKG).
