What to eat in Athens

Quick bite Souvlaki (Gyro Pita)

Charcoal-grilled skewered meat or crispy gyro wrapped in warm pita with tomato, onion, and creamy tzatziki—Athens' beloved handheld feast eaten on the street since ancient times.

Main Moussaka

Layers of tender eggplant, spiced minced lamb, and velvety béchamel baked golden—this comforting casserole is the pride of every Athenian taverna table.

Dessert Loukoumades

Golden, pillowy fried dough balls drenched in honey syrup and dusted with cinnamon—an ancient Greek treat still devoured warm at Athens' historic sweet shops.

Getting to Athens

The main gateway is Athens Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport (ATH).
