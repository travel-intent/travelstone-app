  • Phnom Kulen jungle trekHike through sacred rainforest to waterfalls and the ancient River of a Thousand Lingas carved into the riverbed.
  • Tonlé Sap floating village boat tourGlide past stilted houses and floating markets on Southeast Asia's largest freshwater lake.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Siem Reap

Headed to Siem Reap? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Cambodian riel (KHR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in KHR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Cambodia. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Cambodian riel in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Cambodia aren't blocked as suspicious.
