What to eat in Dunedin

Quick bite Dunedin Cheese Rolls

A beloved Otago/Southland institution - toasted white bread rolled around a creamy cheese and onion soup filling, grilled until crisp and gooey.

Main Bluff Oysters (in season, served Dunedin-style)

Plump, briny wild oysters from nearby Bluff, celebrated across Otago as a prized seasonal delicacy, often served simply fried or natural.

Dessert Cadbury/Jaffa-style Chocolate & Cheese Rolls' sweet cousin - actually Dunedin's iconic sweet is the humble Kiwi favourite reinvented: Cinnamon Oyster (a Dunedin bakery classic)

A locally beloved Dunedin bakery treat, sweet and spiced, best enjoyed fresh with a coffee.

Don't miss in Dunedin

  • Otago Peninsula wildlife cruiseSpot albatross, seals, penguins and sometimes whales on the waters around the peninsula.
  • Tunnel Beach & Otago Peninsula coastal walksTrek along dramatic cliffside trails to hidden sandstone coves and wildlife-rich headlands.
  • Otago Harbour boat tourCruise the scenic harbour past historic Larnach Castle and rich coastal birdlife.

Getting to Dunedin

The main gateway is Dunedin Airport (DUD).
