What to eat in Rio de Janeiro

Quick bite Coxinha

A teardrop-shaped, crispy fried croquette stuffed with shredded chicken and creamy catupiry cheese, the ultimate carioca snack bar staple.

Main Feijoada Carioca

Rio's beloved Saturday ritual—a hearty black bean and pork stew served with rice, farofa, and orange slices that brings the whole city together.

Dessert Brigadeiro

Fudgy little balls of condensed milk and chocolate rolled in sprinkles, the irresistible sweet found at every Brazilian birthday and boteco.

Don't miss in Rio de Janeiro

  • Hike up Pão de Açúcar (Sugarloaf Mountain)Scramble or cable-car up granite peaks for sweeping views of Guanabara Bay and the city skyline.
  • Guanabara Bay boat tourCruise past tropical islands and dramatic coastline with Rio's skyline as a backdrop.

Getting to Rio de Janeiro
