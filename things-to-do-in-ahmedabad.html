What to eat in Ahmedabad

Quick bite Fafda Jalebi

This beloved Sunday morning ritual pairs crispy gram-flour fafda with syrupy hot jalebi for the ultimate sweet-savoury combo Ahmedabadis line up for.

Main Gujarati Thali (with Undhiyu in winter)

A soul-satisfying spread of dal, kadhi, shaak, rotli and the iconic winter mixed-vegetable delicacy undhiyu showcases Ahmedabad's legendary vegetarian hospitality.

Dessert Doodh Pak or Basundi with Puri

This fragrant, cardamom-scented condensed milk sweet paired with crisp puri is a festive Gujarati indulgence that locals crave on special occasions.

Getting to Ahmedabad

The main gateway is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD). Arriving by train, the central station is Ahmedabad Junction.
