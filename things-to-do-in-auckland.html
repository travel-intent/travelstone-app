What to eat in Auckland

Quick bite Mince and Cheese Pie

A flaky, golden pastry stuffed with savoury minced beef and melted cheese - the ultimate grab-and-go Kiwi snack found in every Auckland bakery.

Try it at PieFee
Main Green-lipped Mussels (Kaimoana style)

Plump, sweet mussels native to New Zealand waters, steamed in white wine or served on the half shell, showcasing Auckland's superb harbour-fresh seafood.

Try it at Auckland Fish Market
Dessert Hokey Pokey Ice Cream

New Zealand's beloved vanilla ice cream studded with crunchy honeycomb toffee bits, a Kiwi childhood favourite scooped up all over Auckland.

Try it at The Gelato Workshop Morningside

Don't miss in Auckland

  • SkyJump off Sky TowerLeap from one of the Southern Hemisphere's tallest towers for an adrenaline-charged view of the city below.
  • Sailing on the Waitematā HarbourAuckland's nickname 'City of Sails' comes alive as you cruise its sparkling harbour dotted with yachts.
  • Hauraki Gulf whale and dolphin watchingCruise out from downtown Auckland to spot Bryde's whales, orcas, and playful dolphins in these rich waters.

Getting to Auckland

The main gateway is Auckland Airport (AKL). Arriving by train, the central station is Britomart Station.
