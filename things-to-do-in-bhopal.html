What to eat in Bhopal

Quick bite Bhopali Bafla

Boiled and roasted wheat balls dunked in ghee and served with spicy dal, a hearty street favourite with a distinct smoky flavour.

Main Bhopali Gosht Korma

A legacy of the city's Nawabi kitchens, this slow-cooked mutton korma is silky, fragrant with whole spices, and deeply aromatic.

Dessert Bhopali Khoya Jalebi / Paan

Bhopal's thick, khoya-laden jalebis are syrupy, rich, and utterly indulgent, often paired with a sweet Bhopali paan to finish a meal.

Getting to Bhopal

The main gateway is Raja Bhoj Airport (BHO). Arriving by train, the central station is Bhopal Junction.
