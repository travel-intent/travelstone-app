What to eat in Santiago

Quick bite Completo Italiano

Santiago's beloved hot dog piled high with mashed avocado, tomato, and mayo, echoing the colors of the Italian flag.

Main Pastel de Choclo

A comforting baked corn casserole with a golden sweet-corn crust hiding a savory beef, chicken, olive, and egg filling beneath.

Dessert Mote con Huesillo

A refreshing summer drink-dessert of husked wheat and rehydrated dried peaches swimming in their sweet syrup, sold from street carts across the city.

Don't miss in Santiago

  • Day hike in Cajón del MaipoTrek through dramatic Andean canyons just outside the city, with views of glaciers and volcanic peaks.
  • Ski or climb on nearby Andean volcanoesSantiago's proximity to the Andes makes it a launchpad for volcano and high-altitude excursions like Maipo Volcano.
  • White-water rafting on the Maipo RiverTackle thrilling rapids carved through the Andes foothills just an hour from the capital.

Getting to Santiago

The main gateway is Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCL).
