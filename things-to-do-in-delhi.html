What to eat in Delhi

Quick bite Chole Bhature

Fluffy, deep-fried bhature paired with spicy, tangy chickpea curry is the ultimate hearty Delhi breakfast obsession.

Try it at Sita Ram Diwan Chand
Main Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Delhi's world-famous invention of smoky tandoori chicken simmered in a rich, buttery tomato gravy that's pure comfort on a plate.

Try it at Gulati Restaurant, Pandara Road
Dessert Jalebi (Old Delhi style)

Piping-hot, syrup-soaked coils of crispy batter, best eaten fresh off the kadhai at a bustling Chandni Chowk stall.

Try it at Old Famous Jalebi Wala

Getting to Delhi

The main gateway is Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL). Arriving by train, the central station is New Delhi Railway Station.
