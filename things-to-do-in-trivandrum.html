What to eat in Trivandrum

Quick bite Kappa Vevichathu with Meen Curry

Steamed tapioca mashed and served with fiery fish curry, a Trivandrum comfort classic sold at humble toddy shops and homes alike.

Main Karimeen Pollichathu

Pearl spot fish marinated in tangy spices, wrapped in banana leaf and pan-seared to smoky perfection, a true taste of Kerala's backwaters.

Dessert Palada Pradhaman

Silky rice ada simmered slowly in sweetened milk and ghee, a beloved Onam sadya finale that melts in your mouth.

Getting to Trivandrum

The main gateway is Trivandrum International Airport (TRV). Arriving by train, the central station is Thiruvananthapuram Central.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Trivandrum

Headed to Trivandrum? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Indian rupee (INR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in INR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in India. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Indian rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in India aren't blocked as suspicious.
