What to eat in Montreal

Quick bite Montreal Smoked Meat Sandwich

Piled high on rye with mustard, this peppery, hand-cut smoked brisket from delis like Schwartz's is a city obsession.

Main Poutine

Crispy fries drowned in rich gravy and squeaky cheese curds — Quebec's ultimate comfort food, invented in this very province.

Dessert Montreal-style Bagel (with maple butter)

Hand-rolled, wood-fired, and slightly sweet, these dense bagels are best enjoyed warm with a slick of maple butter.

Getting to Montreal

The main gateway is Montréal-Trudeau International Airport (YUL). Arriving by train, the central station is Gare Centrale.
