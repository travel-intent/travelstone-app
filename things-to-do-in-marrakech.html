Don't miss in Marrakech

  • Overnight camel trek into the Agafay or Sahara desertRide camels into the desert dunes or rocky Agafay landscape and sleep under the stars in a Berber camp.
  • Trekking in the Atlas MountainsDay trips or multi-day hikes to Berber villages and up Mount Toubkal, North Africa's highest peak, just outside the city.
  • Hot-air balloon ride over the Marrakech plainsFloat above palm groves and the Atlas Mountains foothills at sunrise for sweeping desert views.

Getting to Marrakech

The main gateway is Marrakesh Menara Airport (RAK).
