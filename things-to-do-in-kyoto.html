What to eat in Kyoto

Quick bite Yatsuhashi (fresh nama-yatsuhashi)

Soft, cinnamon-scented rice-flour triangles folded around sweet red bean paste, sold warm by Kyoto's street vendors and temple stalls.

Try it at Nishio Tametada Shoten Shin-Kyogoku Store
Main Yudofu

Delicate simmered tofu in a kombu broth, a serene Zen-temple classic that showcases Kyoto's famously pure water and refined subtlety.

Try it at Yudofu Sagano
Dessert Matcha Warabi Mochi / Uji Matcha sweets

Silky, jiggly mochi dusted in kinako and drizzled with rich Uji matcha, capturing Kyoto's legendary green tea culture in every bite.

Try it at Torori Tenshi no Warabi-mochi - Kyoto Kawaramachi Shop

Don't miss in Kyoto

  • Fushimi Inari torii-gate trail hikeWind up Mount Inari through thousands of vermillion torii gates for sweeping views over Kyoto.
  • Hozugawa River boat rideRide a traditional wooden boat through scenic gorges and rapids near Arashiyama, guided by skilled boatmen.

Getting to Kyoto

The main gateway is Kansai International Airport (KIX). Arriving by train, the central station is Kyoto Station.
