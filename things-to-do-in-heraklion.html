What to eat in Heraklion

Quick bite Kalitsounia (Cretan cheese/herb pies)

Golden hand pies stuffed with soft mizithra cheese or wild greens, sold fresh from Heraklion's bakeries and markets.

Main Kreatotourta (Cretan meat pie) / Antikristo lamb

Slow-cooked lamb, often roasted upright by the fire in traditional antikristo style, embodies Crete's rustic mountain feasting.

Dessert Xerotigana / Kalitsounia me meli

Crisp Cretan pastry rolls or sweet cheese pies drizzled with local thyme honey, a beloved finish to any Cretan meal.

Don't miss in Heraklion

  • Trekking the Samaria GorgeOne of Europe's longest gorges, a classic day-long hike through Crete's White Mountains reachable from Heraklion.
  • Snorkelling the clear coves near HeraklionCrete's limestone coastline offers vivid blue waters perfect for exploring hidden coves and reefs.
  • Boat trips to Dia IslandSail from Heraklion's harbor to the nearby uninhabited island for swimming and snorkeling in turquoise bays.
