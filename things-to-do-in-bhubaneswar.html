What to eat in Bhubaneswar

Quick bite Gupchup (Odisha-style Pani Puri)

Crispy hollow puris filled with tangy tamarind water and mashed potato—a beloved street-side ritual across Bhubaneswar.

Main Dalma

A comforting Odia staple of lentils slow-cooked with vegetables and a subtle tempering of panch phoron, served with steamed rice.

Dessert Chhena Poda

Odisha's beloved 'burnt cheese' dessert—caramelized cottage cheese baked to smoky, sweet perfection, born right in this region.

Getting to Bhubaneswar

The main gateway is Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI). Arriving by train, the central station is Bhubaneswar Railway Station.
