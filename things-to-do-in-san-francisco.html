What to eat in San Francisco

Quick bite Clam Chowder in a Sourdough Bread Bowl

Creamy, hearty chowder served in a tangy, chewy sourdough bowl - a Fisherman's Wharf classic you can't leave the city without trying.

Main Dungeness Crab (Crab Louie or whole cracked crab)

Sweet, freshly caught local crab, often served chilled with a zesty Louie dressing or simply cracked with drawn butter down at Fisherman's Wharf.

Dessert Ghirardelli Hot Fudge Sundae

A rich, velvety chocolate sundae from the historic Ghirardelli Square that's been a beloved treat since the 19th century.

Don't miss in San Francisco

  • Alcatraz & San Francisco Bay boat tourCruise past the iconic Golden Gate Bridge and the infamous Alcatraz Island on the bay's sparkling waters.
  • Golden Gate Bridge & Marin Headlands hikeTrek scenic coastal trails with postcard-perfect views back across the bridge to the city skyline.
  • Half Moon Bay whale-watching excursionSpot migrating gray and humpback whales just off the dramatic Northern California coastline.

Getting to San Francisco

The main gateway is San Francisco International Airport (SFO).
