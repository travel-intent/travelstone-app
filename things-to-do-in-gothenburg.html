What to eat in Gothenburg

Quick bite Räkmacka

An open-faced shrimp sandwich piled high with hand-peeled shrimp, mayo, egg and dill—Gothenburg's beloved harbourside snack.

Main Stekt Strömming med Potatismos

Crispy fried Baltic herring served with creamy mashed potatoes and lingonberries, a true West Coast seafood classic.

Dessert Kanelbulle

Sweden's iconic cinnamon bun, best enjoyed warm during a Gothenburg fika with a strong cup of coffee.

Don't miss in Gothenburg

  • Archipelago boat tourHop among the car-free islands of the southern Gothenburg archipelago by ferry or guided boat for a classic coastal escape.
  • Koster Sea safari (day trip from Gothenburg)Join a seal- and marine-life safari into the nearby Koster-Väderöarna marine reserve, a popular day excursion from the city.
  • Paddan canal boat tourGlide beneath Gothenburg's low bridges and through its historic moat canals for a classic sightseeing cruise of the city.

Getting to Gothenburg

The main gateway is Göteborg Landvetter Airport (GOT). Arriving by train, the central station is Göteborg Central Station.
