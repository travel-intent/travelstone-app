What to eat in Odense

Quick bite Smørrebrød

Open-faced rye bread piled high with toppings like pickled herring or roast beef, a quintessential Danish lunch you'll find in every Odense café.

Main Stegt Flæsk med Persillesovs

Denmark's national dish of crispy fried pork slices smothered in creamy parsley sauce and served with boiled potatoes, cherished in traditional Odense kitchens.

Dessert Æbleskiver

Fluffy round pancake puffs dusted with powdered sugar and dipped in jam, a beloved treat especially around Christmastime.
