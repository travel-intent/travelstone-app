What to eat in Edinburgh

Quick bite Scotch Pie

A hand-sized, double-crust savoury pie packed with peppery minced mutton, perfect for eating on the go.

Main Haggis, Neeps and Tatties

Scotland's beloved savoury pudding of spiced offal and oats, served proudly with mashed swede and potatoes.

Dessert Edinburgh Rock

This pastel-coloured, crumbly sugar candy has been melting in Edinburgh mouths since the Victorian era.

Getting to Edinburgh

The main gateway is Edinburgh Airport (EDI). Arriving by train, the central station is Edinburgh Waverley.
