What to eat in Gdańsk

Quick bite Zapiekanka

This toasted half-baguette piled with mushrooms, cheese, and ketchup was born in Poland and remains the ultimate late-night street snack, especially at Gdańsk's famous Bar Turystyczny stalls.

Main Śledź w oleju (Baltic Herring)

Gdańsk's deep ties to the Baltic Sea shine through in this classic herring dish, marinated in oil with onions and served on hearty rye bread, a true taste of the Tri-City coast.

Dessert Piernik Gdański (Gdańsk Gingerbread)

A rich, spiced gingerbread often layered with plum jam and chocolate, this treat carries centuries of Hanseatic trade history in every bite.

Getting to Gdańsk

The main gateway is Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa Airport (GDN). Arriving by train, the central station is Gdańsk Główny.
