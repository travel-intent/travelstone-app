What to eat in Granada

Quick bite Habas con Jamón (as a free tapa)

Tender broad beans stewed with cured Trevélez ham, the quintessential little plate that arrives free with a drink in Granada's legendary tapas bars.

Main Tortilla del Sacromonte

Granada's own gutsy omelette packed with lamb brains and sweetbreads, a Sacromonte gypsy-quarter classic that pairs perfectly with the city's famous free tapas culture.

Dessert Piononos de Santa Fe

A tiny, syrup-soaked rolled sponge cake crowned with toasted cream, born in the nearby town of Santa Fe and adored across Granada.

Don't miss in Granada

  • Trekking in the Sierra NevadaHike the peaks and trails of mainland Spain's highest mountain range, just outside the city.

Getting to Granada

The main gateway is Federico García Lorca Granada Airport (GRX). Arriving by train, the central station is Granada Station.
