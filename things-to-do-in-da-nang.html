What to eat in Đà Nẵng

Quick bite Bánh Tráng Cuốn Thịt Heo

Rice paper rolls packed with grilled pork, herbs, and pickles, dipped in a savory fermented anchovy sauce that locals swear by.

Try it at BÁNH TRÁNG THỊT HEO ĐẠI LỘC - QUÁN NÀNG THƠ 3 - 103 Trưng Nữ Vương
Main Mì Quảng

Đà Nẵng's beloved turmeric-tinted noodle bowl loaded with shrimp, pork, quail eggs, peanuts, and crispy rice crackers in a rich, minimal broth.

Try it at Mì Quảng Sứa Hồng Nga
Dessert Bánh Xèo (Chè Xoa Xoa Hạt Lựu variant aside) — Chè Xoa Xoa Hạt Lựu

A refreshing central Vietnamese sweet with chewy tapioca 'pomegranate seeds' and jelly strands in coconut milk, perfect after a day exploring the beaches.

Try it at Xoa Xoa Hạt Lựu - Chè Local Dessert 째

Don't miss in Đà Nẵng

  • Marble Mountains cave templesExplore hidden cave shrines and tunnels carved into five marble-and-limestone peaks just outside the city.
  • Han River cruise under the Dragon BridgeCruise the river at night to watch the fire-breathing Dragon Bridge light up the skyline.
  • Son Tra Peninsula trekHike forested trails on the 'Monkey Mountain' peninsula for panoramic views of the coastline and rare red-shanked douc langurs.

Getting to Đà Nẵng

The main gateway is Da Nang International Airport (DAD). Arriving by train, the central station is Da Nang Station.
