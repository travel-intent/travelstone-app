What to eat in Graz

Quick bite Käsekrainer

A juicy grilled sausage oozing melted cheese, grabbed hot from a Würstelstand on Graz's streets.

Main Steirisches Backhendl

Styria's beloved crispy fried chicken, golden and tender, best enjoyed with a glass of local pumpkin-seed-oil-dressed salad on the side.

Dessert Steirischer Kernöl-Topfenknödel

Soft curd-cheese dumplings drizzled with dark, nutty Styrian pumpkin seed oil for a sweet-savory Graz specialty.

Getting to Graz

The main gateway is Graz Airport (GRZ). Arriving by train, the central station is Graz Hauptbahnhof.
