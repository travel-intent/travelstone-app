What to eat in Interlaken

Quick bite Bratwurst mit Rösti

A juicy grilled Swiss sausage served alongside golden, crispy pan-fried rösti—simple, hearty street-side comfort food.

Main Cheese Fondue

A bubbling pot of melted Swiss Alpine cheeses shared at the table, best enjoyed after a day among the mountains.

Dessert Meringue with Double Cream (Meringues de l'Etivaz)

A crisp, airy meringue piled high with luscious whipped cream from the nearby Alps—an Interlaken region classic.

Don't miss in Interlaken

  • Skydiving & paragliding over the AlpsTandem paragliding from Harder Kulm or Beatenberg gives jaw-dropping views of Interlaken nestled between two lakes.
  • Jungfrau region alpine hikingTrails beneath the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau peaks offer some of the most dramatic high-alpine scenery in Switzerland.
  • White-water rafting on the Lütschine RiverGlacier-fed rapids rushing through a steep alpine valley make for an adrenaline-packed ride.

Getting to Interlaken

The main gateway is Bern Airport (BRN).
