What to eat in Florianópolis

Quick bite Ostras Fritas (Fried Oysters)

Crispy golden oysters fresh from Florianópolis's famous oyster farms, a beloved snack along the island's beachside kiosks.

Main Camarão na Moranga (Ostras e Camarão à Catarinense)

Plump Santa Catarina shrimp and oysters bathed in a creamy sauce, often served inside a roasted pumpkin, celebrating the island's Azorean seafood heritage.

Dessert Cuca de Banana com Melado

A soft, streusel-topped cake infused with Azorean tradition, drizzled with sweet cane syrup for a comforting finish.

Don't miss in Florianópolis

  • Lagoa da Conceição watersportsPaddleboard, kayak, or sail the lively central lagoon ringed by dunes and hills.
  • Trail to Praia do Rosa and Costa da LagoaHike scenic coastal trails connecting hidden beaches and fishing villages only reachable on foot or by boat.
  • Southern right whale watching near Praia do RosaSpot whales migrating offshore between June and November along Santa Catarina's dramatic coastline.

Getting to Florianópolis

The main gateway is Hercílio Luz International Airport (FLN).
