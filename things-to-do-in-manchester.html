What to eat in Manchester

Quick bite Manchester Egg

A Scotch-egg style pub snack born in the city's Chorlton bars, with a soft-boiled egg wrapped in spiced pork and black pudding.

Main Lancashire Hotpot

A slow-cooked, comforting stew of lamb and onions topped with crispy sliced potatoes, a true taste of the North West.

Dessert Manchester Tart

A retro school-dinner favourite revived by top bakeries, layering raspberry jam, custard and coconut in shortcrust pastry.

Getting to Manchester

The main gateway is Manchester Airport (MAN). Arriving by train, the central station is Manchester Piccadilly.
