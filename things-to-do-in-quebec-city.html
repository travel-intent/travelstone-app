What to eat in Quebec City

Quick bite Poutine

Crispy fries drenched in rich gravy and squeaky cheese curds - this Quebec-born classic is the ultimate late-night comfort food.

Main Tourtière

This spiced, flaky meat pie of ground pork, beef or game is the soul-warming centerpiece of Québécois holiday tables and cozy bistros alike.

Dessert Queen Elizabeth Cake (Gâteau Reine-Élizabeth)

A moist date-and-walnut cake blanketed in broiled coconut-brown sugar topping, a beloved old-Quebec tradition passed down through generations.

Don't miss in Quebec City

  • Ice canoeing on the St. Lawrence RiverA thrilling winter tradition where paddlers navigate icy river waters and ice floes by canoe, unique to the Quebec region.
  • Snowmobiling through the Laurentian wildernessQuebec City is a gateway to vast snowmobile trail networks winding through snowy forests and rolling hills.
  • Exploring Montmorency Falls and Jacques-Cartier National ParkHike trails with suspension bridges overlooking falls taller than Niagara, or trek through dramatic river canyons nearby.

Getting to Quebec City

The main gateway is Québec City Jean Lesage International Airport (YQB). Arriving by train, the central station is Gare du Palais.
