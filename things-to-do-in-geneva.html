What to eat in Geneva

Quick bite Longeole sausage in a sandwich

A Geneva specialty pork sausage spiced with fennel seeds, traditionally grilled and tucked into bread for a quick, savory street bite.

Main Filets de perche du Léman

Delicate, golden-fried fillets of perch straight from Lake Geneva, served with a squeeze of lemon and tartar sauce at a lakeside terrace.

Dessert Chocolat Genevois (Geneva chocolates/truffles)

Rich, melt-in-the-mouth pralines and truffles from Geneva's legendary chocolatiers like Stettler and Rohr, best enjoyed with a coffee by the lake.

Don't miss in Geneva

  • Lake Geneva boat cruiseGlide past the Jet d'Eau and lakeside mansions with views to the Alps beyond.
  • Day hike in the nearby Alps (Mont Salève or Chamonix)Just a short trip from the city, trails offer sweeping views of Mont Blanc and the Geneva basin.

Getting to Geneva

The main gateway is Geneva International Airport (GVA).
