What to eat in Venice

Quick bite Cicchetti (with baccalà mantecato)

Venice's beloved bar snacks—little bites like creamy whipped salt-cod on crostini, best enjoyed with a glass of local wine at a bacaro.

Main Sarde in Saor

Sweet-and-sour marinated sardines with onions, pine nuts, and raisins—an iconic Venetian dish born from centuries-old sailors' preservation techniques.

Dessert Tiramisù

Though claimed by the Veneto region broadly, Venice proudly serves this luscious layered coffee-and-mascarpone classic as a fitting sweet finale.

Don't miss in Venice

  • Gondola ride through the canalsGlide past centuries-old palazzos on a classic gondola steered by a singing gondolier.
