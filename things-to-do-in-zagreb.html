What to eat in Zagreb

Quick bite Burek

Flaky, spiral-layered pastry filled with meat or cheese, grabbed hot from a bakery window on the way to work.

Main Zagrebački odrezak (Zagreb steak)

A crumbed veal or pork cutlet stuffed with ham and cheese, fried golden and oozing when cut open.

Dessert Zagrebačke šnite (Zagreb cutlets/cremeschnitte)

A rich vanilla custard slice with flaky pastry layers that locals swear is best from the old-town bakeries.

Getting to Zagreb

The main gateway is Zagreb Airport (ZAG). Arriving by train, the central station is Zagreb Glavni kolodvor.
