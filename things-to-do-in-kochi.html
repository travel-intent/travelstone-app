What to eat in Kochi

Quick bite Kappa and Meen Curry / Kozhikodan-style Banana Chips

Crispy golden banana chips fried in coconut oil make for the perfect crunchy Kochi street snack.

Main Kerala Fish Curry (Meen Vevichathu)

Tangy, fiery red fish curry simmered with kudampuli and coconut oil, best mopped up with red rice or kappa.

Dessert Palada Payasam

A silky, cardamom-scented rice-flake and milk pudding that graces every Kerala festival sadya.

Getting to Kochi

The main gateway is Cochin International Airport (COK). Arriving by train, the central station is Ernakulam Junction.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Kochi

Headed to Kochi? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in Indian rupee (INR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in INR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in India. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little Indian rupee in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in India aren't blocked as suspicious.
