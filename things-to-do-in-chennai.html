What to eat in Chennai

Quick bite Sundal

This tangy, spiced chickpea or lentil snack sold by beachside vendors on Marina Beach is a quintessential Chennai evening ritual.

Main Chettinad Chicken Curry

A fiery, aromatic classic loaded with freshly ground spices that showcases Tamil Nadu's most celebrated culinary tradition.

Dessert Mysore Pak

This melt-in-your-mouth ghee-soaked sweet, though born in Mysore, is a beloved staple in every Chennai sweet shop and celebration.

Getting to Chennai

The main gateway is Chennai International Airport (MAA). Arriving by train, the central station is MGR Chennai Central.
