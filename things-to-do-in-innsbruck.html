What to eat in Innsbruck

Quick bite Tiroler Speck mit Brot

Delicately smoked, air-cured Tyrolean ham served simply on rustic bread, a beloved alpine snack straight from the mountain farms.

Main Tiroler Gröstl

A hearty skillet of pan-fried potatoes, onions and leftover roast meat topped with a fried egg, the ultimate after-ski comfort dish.

Dessert Kaiserschmarrn

Fluffy shredded caramelized pancake dusted with powdered sugar and served with tart plum compote, an Austrian classic loved across Tyrol.

Don't miss in Innsbruck

  • Nordkette alpine hiking above the cityHop a cable car straight from downtown into the Karwendel mountains for dramatic ridge hikes and panoramic views over Innsbruck.
  • Skiing and snow adventures in the surrounding AlpsInnsbruck's Olympic ski heritage puts world-class slopes and snow activities just minutes from the city center.
  • Swarovski Crystal Worlds and nearby alpine cavesExplore glittering grotto-like chambers and the region's karst caves as a quirky counterpoint to Innsbruck's mountain scenery.

Getting to Innsbruck

The main gateway is Innsbruck Airport (INN). Arriving by train, the central station is Innsbruck Hauptbahnhof.

How to avoid foreign transaction fees in Innsbruck

Headed to Innsbruck? Here's how to avoid foreign transaction fees (also called international transaction fees) on the trip. You'll pay in euro (EUR), and while cards work almost everywhere, many home-country cards quietly add about 1–3% to every purchase abroad. Four ways to keep that money:

  • Always pay in EUR, never your home currency. If a card terminal offers to charge you in your own currency ("dynamic currency conversion"), decline — that built-in rate is usually worse than your bank's.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fee in Austria. Many travel and digital-bank cards waive the 1–3% charge entirely.
  • Withdraw and carry a little euro in cash for markets, small vendors and tips, and skip ATMs that push a conversion on you.
  • Tell your bank your travel dates so your payments in Austria aren't blocked as suspicious.
