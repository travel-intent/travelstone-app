What to eat in Bangkok

Quick bite Pad Thai (from a street cart)

Smoky wok-fried rice noodles with tamarind, shrimp, tofu and peanuts — the iconic Bangkok street snack every traveller hunts down.

Try it at Padthai
Main Pad Kra Pao (Stir-fried Holy Basil with Minced Pork/Chicken and Fried Egg)

A fiery, aromatic basil stir-fry topped with a runny fried egg that's the ultimate Bangkok comfort meal.

Try it at Original Pad Kra Pao 1993
Dessert Mango Sticky Rice (Khao Niao Mamuang)

Sweet ripe mango draped over warm coconut-laced sticky rice — Bangkok's most beloved dessert.

Try it at My favorite mango sticky rice(ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วงร้านโปรด)

Getting to Bangkok

The main gateway is Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK). Arriving by train, the central station is Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal.
