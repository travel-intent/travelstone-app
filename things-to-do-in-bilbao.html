What to eat in Bilbao

Quick bite Pintxos (especially Gilda)

Bilbao's beloved bar-hopping bites, with the classic Gilda skewering spicy guindilla peppers, anchovies and olives in one zesty mouthful.

Main Bacalao al Pil-Pil

Silky salt cod bathed in a garlicky, emulsified olive oil sauce that Basque cooks have perfected over generations.

Dessert Pastel Vasco (Basque Cake)

A rustic, buttery pastry filled with pastry cream or cherry jam, cherished across the Basque Country as the ultimate comfort dessert.

Getting to Bilbao

The main gateway is Bilbao Airport (BIO). Arriving by train, the central station is Bilbao-Abando.
