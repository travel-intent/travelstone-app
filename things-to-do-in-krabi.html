What to eat in Krabi

Quick bite Moo Ping (Grilled Pork Skewers)

Smoky, caramelized marinated pork skewers grilled streetside, best eaten hot off the coals with sticky rice.

Try it at THAI ME UP PANG Restaurant and Bar
Main Gaeng Som Pla (Southern Sour Curry with Fish)

A fiery, tangy turmeric-laced curry loaded with fresh local fish and vegetables, capturing the bold soul of southern Thai coastal cooking.

Try it at Dang kao restaurant
Dessert Khanom Krok

Crispy-edged coconut rice pancakes cooked in a special cast-iron pan, served hot for a sweet-savoury bite by the sea.

Try it at ขนมครก ถ้ำเสือ

Don't miss in Krabi

  • Longtail boat tour to Phi Phi & Railay beachesCruise turquoise waters past dramatic limestone karsts to secluded beaches only reachable by boat.
  • Hong Island & Emerald Cave explorationKayak or swim through a hidden cave tunnel that opens into a lush, hidden lagoon surrounded by cliffs.
  • Snorkelling around the Krabi islandsDiscover vibrant coral reefs and marine life in the clear waters surrounding Koh Poda and nearby islets.

Getting to Krabi

The main gateway is Krabi International Airport (KBV).
