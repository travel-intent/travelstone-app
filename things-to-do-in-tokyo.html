What to eat in Tokyo

Quick bite Tsukiji-style Tamagoyaki on a stick

A pillowy, slightly sweet rolled omelette grilled fresh and handed to you piping hot at the old Tsukiji outer market stalls.

Try it at Marutake
Main Edomae Sushi

Tokyo's original hand-pressed sushi style, born in Edo-era stalls, showcasing lightly cured, vinegar-kissed neta over warm shari.

Try it at Edomae Sushi Hattori
Dessert Ningyo-yaki

Small castella-cake sweets shaped like little dolls or lanterns and filled with sweet red bean paste, a beloved Asakusa treat.

Try it at Kimura Ningyōyaki

Getting to Tokyo

The main gateway is Haneda Airport (HND). Arriving by train, the central station is Tokyo Station.
