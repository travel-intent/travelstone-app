What to eat in Tromsø

Quick bite Fiskekaker

Warm, pan-fried fish cakes sold from harbourside stalls, best eaten straight away with a squeeze of lemon.

Main Bacalao

A rich stew of salted cod, tomatoes, olives and potatoes that showcases Northern Norway's deep cod-fishing heritage.

Dessert Multekrem

Golden cloudberries folded into whipped cream—a delicate, tangy-sweet taste of the Arctic wilderness.

Don't miss in Tromsø

  • Northern lights chaseHead out by minibus or boat into the dark Arctic countryside to hunt the aurora borealis dancing overhead.
  • Orca and humpback safari in the fjordsCruise out from Tromsø into nearby fjords to watch pods of orcas and humpback whales feeding in winter waters.
  • Snowmobile safari across the Lyngen AlpsRace over snow-covered plateaus and frozen valleys beneath Tromsø's dramatic arctic peaks.

Getting to Tromsø

The main gateway is Tromsø Airport (TOS).
